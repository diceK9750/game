# Character atlas generation

Created with the built-in image_gen tool, not the CLI. No external character references were supplied.
Final selected files: rin-atlas.png and koh-atlas.png. Both are 1536x1024 expression atlases;
512x512 cells, three columns and two rows. Reading order: idle, celebrate, victory, hurt, frustrated, defeat.
The separate attempted transparent rabbit edit was rejected and is not shipped.

## Rin: selected generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production game sprite expression sheet, ONE transparent PNG, 1536x1024, precisely 3 columns by 2 rows of equal 512x512 cells. Six full body drawings of THE SAME original two-head-tall rabbit player character, identical costume proportions and scale. The character is RIN, a cream-white rabbit with very large expressive amber eyes, soft pink inner ears with one relaxed ear tip, tiny tuft of cream forehead fur, short cyan adventure jacket over a navy romper, flowing mint scarf, small golden round lantern pin, compact padded boots and mitt-like rabbit paws. Cheerful, confident, appealing to all ages; NOT a recognizable existing franchise character. Modern premium hand-painted 2.5D game mascot rendering: beautifully controlled warm fur shading, crisp readable silhouette, tactile fabric, subtle rim light, large facial features and small body, no heavy black outlines, no pixel art, no photorealism. Slight three-quarter view toward RIGHT (this character stands left of the game board). Equal-size characters centered in each cell; highest ear around y=30 of its cell, boots around y=465; entire body, ears, arms and scarf within cell and at least 25px padding. EXACT six poses reading left-to-right:
top left idle: lively open eyes, relaxed smile, ready stance.
top middle celebrate: delighted open-mouth smile, both paws lifted beside face.
top right victory: beaming proud smile, fist upward, one small gold star above paw.
bottom left hurt: surprised rounded mouth, ears tilted back, paws near chest, no injuries.
bottom middle frustrated: eyebrows raised with determined pout, hands on hips.
bottom right defeat: gentle disappointed expression, lowered ears, shoulders slumped but still standing.
Fully transparent alpha background. No ground, floor, drop shadow, scenery, labels, letters, grids, borders, numbers or watermark. All six sprites separated with transparent gutters, same visual scale, same character identity and face. Production-ready sprites, not a concept presentation.
